The problem

You can check the menu. You can check the photos. You can't check the noise.

So you guess. And some nights you spend the whole dinner shouting.

How we score noise

We score every restaurant on two things:

  • What reviews say about the noise.
  • Real mic readings, taken by people at the venue.

You see both, and how much data is behind the score. Two readings and fifty readings look different in the app.

The honest bit

Noise changes. A busy Friday is louder than a slow Tuesday. Our score tells you what to expect.

We cover Sydney, plus Newcastle, Dubbo, Moss Vale and Kiama. Every reading makes it better.

Questions

What is cafequiet?

cafequiet is an app that ranks Sydney restaurants by how quiet they are.

How does cafequiet measure noise?

Two signals: mentions of noise in restaurant reviews, and microphone readings taken by cafequiet users at the venue.

Is the noise score accurate?

It's an indicator, not a guarantee. A restaurant's noise level changes with the night and the crowd, so the score tells you what to expect.

Which areas does cafequiet cover?

Greater Sydney, plus Newcastle, Dubbo, Moss Vale and Kiama.

Can I use cafequiet now?

It's in closed beta. You need a referral code to get in, and you can request early access on the site.
