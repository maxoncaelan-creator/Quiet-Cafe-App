## Process

1. Confirm the target city with Caelan.
2. Find data sources for restaurants in that city.
3. Find data sources for noise: reviews that mention noise, decibel or
   crowdsourced sound data, and existing signals such as popular-times or
   noise indicators.
4. List what each source gives, and what it needs (API key, rate limit, or
   scraping).

## Review gate

carrying into ranking design before stage 02 starts.
